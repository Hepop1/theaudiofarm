03 — The studio

Like many great things, it started in a pub.

After one meeting, Lukas, Alex and Sam set off to build a studio. Sam already had the desk — they just needed somewhere to put it. Then they found The Hawthorns in Staunton: a perfect little getaway in rural Gloucestershire. Nearly a year of building later, it was ready.

Out of the way on a working farm, it feels isolated in the best sense — nothing from outside creeping in, just you and the song.

Location The Hawthorns Estate, Staunton, Gloucester
